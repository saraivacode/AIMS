Generated by `fl_main.py:run_security_sensitivity_epochs()` via `--security-sensitivity-epochs`.

**Attack:** label-flip (Critical -> Adequate), 1 malicious client out of 3, NonIID.
**Local epochs tested:** 1, 3, 5, 10.
**Defenses:** FedAvg, Krum, TrimmedMean.
